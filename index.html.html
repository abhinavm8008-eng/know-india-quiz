About the Competition

Our Mission

The Know India Quiz 2026 is designed to foster pride and curiosity about India's diverse heritage, rich artforms, historical milestones, and modern advancements among students nationwide.

Eligibility

Open to school and institution students across India. Participants register individually using a valid Gmail account with fast OTP verification.

Official Digital E-Certificates

All verified participants who complete the Online Prelims will receive an official Digital E-Certificate of Participation. Finalists receive E-Certificates of Merit.

Quiz Syllabus & Topics

1. Indian History & Freedom Movement

Revolutionaries, freedom struggle timeline, ancient kingdoms, and architectural heritage.

2. Classical & Folk Artforms

Traditional paintings (Madhubani, Warli, Tanjore), classical dances, music, and handicraft traditions.

3. Geography & Natural Wonders

Rivers, mountain ranges, national parks, UNESCO heritage sites, and climate diversity.

4. Science, ISRO & Defence

India's space exploration missions, scientific achievements, innovators, and technological milestones.

5. Culture, Literature & Current Affairs

Indian epics, famous authors, national symbols, modern sports achievements, and civic knowledge.

Competition Schedule

Round 1: Online Prelims

An interactive, timed online quiz accessible from any browser. Test your quick thinking across history, culture, and general knowledge. All eligible participants score here to earn their official E-Certificate.

R2

Round 2: Grand Finale on Zoom

The top 6 highest-scoring participants from Round 1 qualify for the live interactive Grand Finale conducted on Zoom. Compete live in buzzer rounds, visual rounds, and rapid-fire questions!

Frequently Asked Questions

How many rounds are in the quiz?

There are exactly 2 rounds: Round 1 is the Online Prelims, and the Top 6 participants move to Round 2 (Grand Finale on Zoom).

How will I join the Zoom Finale if I qualify?

The top 6 scorers from Round 1 will receive a direct email with the Zoom meeting link and instructions for the live finale.

Is registration free?

Yes! Registration is 100% free for all students.
